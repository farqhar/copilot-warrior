## Observations

- **Release notes go quiet.** Microsoft's official Copilot release notes sometimes went three or more weeks without a new batch. Support pages and product blogs were often fresher.
- **Almost every new feature needed the paid licence** and arrived via phased rollout, so "I can't see it" was a weekly reply. State the licence on every tip.
- **Features ship platform by platform.** Web-only, Windows-only and iPhone-first releases were common. Sanity-check one machine before telling a whole team to try something.
