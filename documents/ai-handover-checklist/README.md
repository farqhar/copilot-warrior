# D3 · AI handover and adoption checklist

Most AI tools in an organisation die the same way. The person who built it moves on, nobody knows the prompt, it was running under their account, and Microsoft renamed the feature. This checklist is the handover that prevents that.

**Licence to use the template:** none · **Last verified:** pending re-test

## Files
- [`template.md`](template.md) is the source of truth.
- [`template.docx`](template.docx) is generated from the Markdown.

## How to use it
- Fill it in **with** the new owner, not for them. The conversation is the point.
- Section 2 ("it doesn't run under a personal account") is the item most often missed and most often fatal.
- Any unticked box needs a reason written next to it.
- Re-run sections 4 and 6 every quarter.

## The Copilot prompt that works best with it
Point Copilot at the agent, skill or workflow's documentation and ask:
> Using this documentation, fill in sections 1 and 3 of the AI handover checklist. For anything the documentation doesn't answer, leave the box unticked and write "Not documented". Don't guess.

The "Not documented" items are your to-do list before handover.
