# D1 · AI project brief

A one-to-two-page brief for any AI idea, from "let's schedule a prompt" to "let's build an agent". It makes people answer the questions that sink AI projects later: *who checks the output?*, *what data does it read?* and *what does it cost?*

**Licence to use the template:** none · **Last verified:** pending re-test

## Files
- [`template.md`](template.md) is the source of truth. Edit this one.
- [`template.docx`](template.docx) is generated from the Markdown for people who live in Word.

## How to fill it in
1. **Sections 1–3 first, without mentioning AI.** If you can't describe today's process, you're not ready to automate it.
2. **Section 4 is the most important.** Split every step into "AI does" and "a person does". If no person checks anything, go back to section 6.
3. **Section 6 is non-negotiable** for anything that acts on someone's behalf, records people, or reads client data.
4. **Section 7:** a rough "today" number beats no number. Self-reported time is fine at the idea stage.

## The Copilot prompt that works best with it
Open the blank template in Word, open Copilot, and paste:
> Using my notes below, fill in sections 1 to 4 of this AI project brief. Leave sections 5 to 9 blank for me. Where my notes don't give you an answer, write "TBC" instead of guessing.
>
> [paste your rough notes, an email or a meeting transcript]

Then complete sections 5–9 yourself. These are judgement calls, not drafting.
